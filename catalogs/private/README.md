# Private-source catalogs

The catalogs and maps here are built on rooms transcribed from the maintainer's private campaign battle-map art
(GENERAL_ENV.md "Hand maps").

**What may be published (the maintainer, 2026-11-17):** the room layouts (walls and obstacles, with their neutral
ids such as `mausoleum-forecourt`) may be served in the web game Forests and Faeries, and the catalogs
that use them may be used with it.

**What stays private:** the source art, the overlay images and any campaign names or lore. They live
outside Git (`<offline-results>`); never add them here, and never put campaign
names or lore into ids, map files or catalog rows.
