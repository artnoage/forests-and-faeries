# The Tall Country: provenance of the generated media

Every image, voice, music and video file in `media/` was generated for this story by Claude (Anthropic) through paid APIs on the maintainer's accounts, on the dates below, and then re-encoded with FFmpeg (stills to WebP, narration and music to MP3, clips to H.264 + AAC; metadata stripped). No stock or third-party assets. Prompts name no living artist, studio or existing franchise.

Story, script and narration text: written by Claude for this game (original setting; monsters are SRD 5.2.1 creatures, heroes are the game's own). The narrator is Mara, in the voice her battle lines use. Scene stills that show the cast were painted with watercolour reference sheets; the heroes' sheets were redrawn from the game's own hero figure art (image edit), so the stills match the figures; the ogre, owlbear and Skarrow sheets were drawn from text (all listed at the end). Clips are image-to-video from the scene's own still.

Prompt hash: the first 12 hex digits of the SHA-256 of the exact prompt (or narration text) sent. Cost: from the response usage at list prices, USD counted 1:1 as EUR (the ledger is in the private repo).
