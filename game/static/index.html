FORESTS AND FAERIES · HOW TO PLAY

How to play

  1. Choose a difficulty and level. Pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Unbeatable (levels no language-model player won and where both game AIs won fewer than half of their 500 games), or open Classic for the eleven earlier ungraded fights from The Crypt and The Vault. ✦ Levels reopens the chooser at any time. Fight uses the suggested layout and rolls initiative at once; Place the figures lets you put each figure on a square first (select it, then click a highlighted square), then press Roll initiative and start.
  2. Take your turn. On your turn you have one action, one bonus action and your movement. You regain your reaction at the start of your turn; a trigger can let you use it on your turn or someone else's. The cards at the bottom are everything the engine allows right now. Pick a card, then a highlighted creature or square. Moving is a card too: teal squares are reachable, and the tooltip shows the cost. A path that leaves a conscious enemy's reach highlights orange and asks you to Confirm (using Disengage, or a Champion's Remarkable Athlete free move after a critical hit, protects the move). Hidden foes are revealed automatically as soon as a hero can see them clearly. Several moves and a bonus action can follow one another. Press End Turn when you are done.
  3. Choices, cover and warnings. One card stands for each ability: a spell cast with a damage type you pick (Chromatic Orb, Sorcerous Burst, Dragon's Breath) shows Damage type pills, a dagger shows Melee or Thrown, and the card remembers your last pick for the rest of the fight. A spell with several slot levels shows Slot pills; a sorcerer's Metamagic, or a feature with modes, shows Metamagic or Mode pills. Every choice shows its cost, target damage defenses (takes double, resists or immune to), and any target cover (+2 half cover or +5 three-quarters cover, traced corner-to-corner). Heals on a cursed (Mummy Rot) ally, or Aid that would grant no Hit Points to an already-aided ally at 0 HP, are marked with a warning.
  4. What a card does, and the character sheet. Hover a card, or reach it with Tab, to read what it does: its cost and uses left, range, area, save or attack roll and dice. On a touch screen, pick the card and press About. Sheet on a party card (or beside the acting figure, or in the inspector) opens the character sheet: AC, hit points, conditions, Dying or Stable with death saves, spell slots and uses left, and every feature, attack and spell. Monsters are in the same panel.
  5. Darts, rays and several targets. Magic Missile (each dart strikes as its own damage instance) and multi-target spells (Bless, Aid, Command — currently Grovel/Halt chosen automatically) ask you to split targets first: click creatures (or use + and −) until the count is full, then press Cast. Scorching Ray is aimed one ray at a time: after each ray resolves, pick the target for the next ray (marked Ray N/M, part of the same cast). Chromatic Orb leaps automatically to another creature within 30 feet when two damage dice show the same number.
  6. Areas. Area spells (Burning Hands, Thunderwave, Shatter…) are aimed in two steps: click the square to aim at, look at the highlighted area, then Confirm. A choice marked ⚠ ally or ⚠ self would also hit one of your figures.
  7. Reactions are currently automatic (as are Bardic dice). In SRD 5.2.1, reactions and Bardic Inspiration are optional; this build currently resolves Opportunity Attacks, Shield, Goblin Boss's Redirect Attack, Bardic Inspiration (when an inspired ally misses an attack or fails a saving throw by 1–6) and Cutting Words (when an enemy non-critical hit beats AC by 0–5, or when enemy damage would drop a standing ally and subtracting up to 6 can keep them standing, applied once to a shared area-spell roll before saves halve it) automatically when they apply.
  8. The monsters' turns play by themselves. Watch lets the engine play your heroes too; ⏸ pauses, ⏭ plays one turn.
  9. Dice. The dice tray shows the engine's own rolls for each step (advantage shows both dice). The log has every roll in words.
  10. No 3D? If WebGL is missing or fails, a 2D map takes its place: click its squares and figures, or use the arrow keys and Enter. The 3D view comes back by itself after a lost context.

Levels

    Markers on the board

    • gold ring: the acting figure
    • red ring and DYING tag: a hero at 0 HP, unconscious, rolling a death save at the start of each turn (green pips: successes, red pips: failures; three successes make it stable, three failures kill it, and every hit it takes at 0 HP is a failure, two on a critical hit); teal ring: stable at 0 HP; dark ring: dead. Healing wakes a hero at 0 HP; Spare the Dying makes it stable.
    • paralysed · poisoned · frightened · cursed (no healing) · Strength drained
    • Bless, Guiding Bolt (the next attack has advantage) · slowed (Ray of Frost) · Shield of Faith, Sanctuary, Protection
    • pale dome and sigil: Sanctuary. Each attacker makes a Wisdom save first: on a success the attack goes ahead, on a failure it is lost. The ward ends when the warded hero makes an attack roll, casts a spell or deals damage, and it does not stop area effects or a monster ability that calls for a saving throw instead of an attack roll (a Wight's Life Drain).
