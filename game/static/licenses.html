About this game

Forests and Faeries is an independent, free fan project: a small tactical tabletop game whose rules come from the open-source Natural20 engine by Joseph Dayo and contributors. The game runs ember-rs, a Rust port of Natural20 that is checked against the original on recorded games, roll by roll; every legal action, roll, hit and result follows Natural20's rules, and the page only shows them.

This game would not exist without Natural20. Joseph Dayo has been building it in the open since 2020, first in Ruby and since 2024 in Python, and shares it under the MIT licence. Thank you, Joseph. If you enjoy Forests and Faeries, please ⭐ star Natural20 on GitHub.

Forests and Faeries is open source too: github.com/artnoage/forests-and-faeries (MIT). A star helps, and requests help even more: tell us what you would like to see in an issue.

Used here: the rules for the heroes' classes, species, feats, spells and features (Guy, Elion, Curogana, Mara, Ilyra and Chruna are original characters built with them), and the stat blocks of the Skeleton, Zombie, Ghoul, Ghast, Shadow, Specter, Wight, Mummy, Goblin Minion, Goblin Warrior, Goblin Boss, Kobold Warrior and Animated Armor. The SRD material is adapted to a limited tactical game with a restricted set of actions; the summaries on the character sheets are paraphrases.

Original art

The level names, maps, character names and descriptions, the interface, the procedural board themes, the dice tray and the SVG portraits are original to this project.

Miniatures. All seventeen figures (Guy, Elion, Curogana, Mara, Ilyra, Chruna, the eight undead, the goblin, the goblin boss and the animated armor) were made for this project in November 2026: front and back reference images from ChatGPT image generation (through OpenAI Codex; Chruna's redrawn from the project's own drawing of her) and Gemini image generation (for the animated armor), meshes from Tripo (multiview image-to-3D), then reduced, rigged and animated in Blender with the project's own armatures and clips.

Mausoleum board textures and props. The stone floor, wall and stained-glass window textures of the mausoleum themes are original images from ChatGPT image generation (through OpenAI Codex), made tileable and converted into normal and roughness maps locally. The sarcophagus and brazier props are procedural Blender models.

All of this was generated with the project's own accounts under the terms that applied at the time; no third-party asset is included in it.
